    Tropical heat reminders (Saint Lucia)

    Food safety: keep butter, cream, eggs and fillings refrigerated (below 5 °C). Do not leave cream, buttercream or strawberry fillings at room temperature for more than 2 hours, or 1 hour in hot weather. Wash hands and surfaces; keep raw eggs apart.
    Refrigeration: chill filled tiers before covering, and keep the finished cake in an air-conditioned or refrigerated space. Allow chilled fondant to come up slowly in a cool room so it doesn't sweat; humidity makes fondant soft and sticky.
    Structure: use a cake board under every tier, dowels or straws in every tier that carries weight, and a centre dowel through the stack. A 14" fruit cake base carries the most load; check it is level.
    Transport: pre-cool the vehicle with A/C, keep the cake flat on a non-slip mat, drive slowly, and avoid midday sun. Transporting tiers separately and stacking at the venue is safest. Bring a repair kit (spare icing, piping bags, palette knife, spare flowers).
    At the venue: set up in shade or indoors, away from windows, heat lamps and direct sun. Confirm with the venue where the cake will stand and for how long before cutting.
